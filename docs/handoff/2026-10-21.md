## Open

  static BSS heap (`build_linux`: `.space 1073741824`; `RAIL_ARENA_MB` has no effect there) and
  never collects before it is full; the box resets rather than the process dying. A Pi verdict on
  the live record needs a Linux heap that collects sooner (an mmap path that honours
  `RAIL_ARENA_MB`), or a smaller record. Until then Linux replay is proven on the 38-frame record.
