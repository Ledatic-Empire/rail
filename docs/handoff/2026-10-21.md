## Open

- **A record with no `epoch_seed` key at all replays as seed 0.** `replay.rail:62` reads
  `json_int (json_get frame "epoch_seed")`, and a missing key comes back 0, not -1, so a doctored
  record without the key was replayed (to a MISMATCH) rather than refused with `NO_PROVENANCE`.
  The beacon always writes the key (-1 when unseeded), so no real pulse hits it; one line in
  rail-plasma makes it loud (`json_has` or a sentinel before `json_int`). Found by the planted
  test, not by a pulse.
