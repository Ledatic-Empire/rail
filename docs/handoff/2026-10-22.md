## Open

- **`tools/linux_libc.s`'s `snprintf` ignores `%.17g`** (new): `show_float_exact` is not a round-trip
  printer on Linux. Nothing on the replay path prints floats as text (the record is judged by bits),
  so this is claimed, not fixed. A fix is a precision-honouring `%g` in the shim, or a Rail printer
  (`float_hex.rail`'s method, decimal) that needs no libc.
- **`tools/v5/link_macho.rail`** (deletion candidate, periodic pass): the standalone runner reads a
  fixed `/tmp/link_src.s`; `tools/independence/link_file.rail` takes paths. One comment in
